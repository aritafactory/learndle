How to play?

You have 6 attempts to guess the hidden word. Letter colors show your progress:

  • Correct place
  • In the word, wrong place
  • Not in the word

Controls:

  • Use your keyboard or the on-screen keys.
  • ENTER submits a word, ⌫ removes a letter.
  • Hints spend your total points.

Hints:

  • Part of speech — shows the word's part of speech.
  • Example sentence — shows usage with the word omitted.
  • Synonym / Antonym — when available.
  • Definition — shows the definition.
  • Show letters — marks the letters contained in the word.
  • Reveal a letter — places a random letter; it can be reused, but its cost increases.
