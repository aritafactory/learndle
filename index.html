How to play

You have 6 tries to guess the hidden word. Letter colors explain your progress:

  • Correct spot
  • In word but wrong spot
  • Not in the word

Interface

  • Use keyboard or on-screen keys to type.
  • ENTER submits, ⌫ deletes.
  • Total Points are shared and can be spent on hints.

Hints

  • Part of Speech — shows part of speech.
  • Example Sentence — word in a sentence.
  • Synonym / Antonym — if available.
  • Definition — word definition.
  • Scramble Letters — shuffle answer letters.
  • Reveal Letter — if no synonym/antonym, opens a random letter; cost increases with each use.
